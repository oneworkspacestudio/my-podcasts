# Sources and coverage

Default directory: BBC Technology, The Verge, TechCrunch, Ars Technica, Google
Blog, Microsoft Blog and NVIDIA Blog. Primary announcements are identified
separately from independent reporting. Current feed availability varies; each
episode records outages and the cutoff. RSS excerpts are bounded and summarized.

The people directory includes Sam Altman, Satya Nadella, Sundar Pichai, Demis
Hassabis, Elon Musk, Andrej Karpathy, Yann LeCun and Mustafa Suleyman, with explicit
profile links. Names and company descriptions are directory context, not a
promise of current employment or access. No private social accounts are accessed.

X's official API requires a developer bearer token with account/timeline access.
It resolves the selected handle, then requests original recent posts with
timestamps. Missing credentials use accessible indexed posts through web search;
this is incomplete, not a full timeline scrape. No paywall or login bypass.
LinkedIn, Threads, private Facebook and authenticated Instagram collection are
not implemented. Public accessible posts may appear through search.

Web research targets Reuters, AP, BBC, FT, WSJ, Bloomberg, The Verge, TechCrunch
and primary company statements. Search availability and publisher restrictions
vary. Search URLs come from provider-reported retrieval/citation metadata;
publication dates absent from that metadata are honestly labelled. The
researcher/editor must distinguish dated recent news from older context.

Initial discovery checked current Reuters reporting syndicated at
[MarketScreener](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/tech-stock-rout-accelerates-retreat-from-the-ai-trade-ce785fddd988fe25)
and [AP's own reporting](https://apnews.com/article/c6dd26d8e67270db543c37ac3762acd9).
Direct X content was not independently accessible during discovery; no precise
social-post claim was substituted from an unofficial mirror. These links are
design research, not permanently embedded "latest" stories or seed episodes.

Exports retain evidence IDs, actual source URLs, research cutoff and editorial
notes. Linking a source does not endorse its claims. Company announcements and
posts remain attributed; corroboration across independent reports is requested.


Provider selection in preview.2: automatic DeepSeek research uses its standalone
Search API and records returned URLs, dates and bounded summaries. Automatic
AI Service research uses only the selected RSS feeds and connected direct X
posts; it performs no indexed web/social search. DeepSeek or OpenAI web research
can be selected separately while the local service writes and reviews. Unknown
publication dates stay marked unknown and cannot support a fresh breaking claim.
