# Editorial draft repair

The production worker previously stopped on the first editorial rejection.
Manual retry discarded the draft and notes and charged another episode slot.
Preview.8 keeps the research and editor’s notes, requests one conservative
revision, then rechecks it before speech. A repair can omit weak claims or
stories and can finish shorter. If the revision still needs attention, its
specific note is shown in the queue and full notes in the episode.

An existing blocked draft can use **Repair draft** with the saved research
even when the research service is disconnected or the daily episode budget
is full. The worker persists the repair checkpoint and stops after one
automatic revision. The selected writer still needs to be available.
Fact, attribution, quote, and impersonation checks continue before recording.

Keep the existing 48-payload native app update contract and DB version.
My Voice and its credentials, schedules, and private intro tracks retain
their existing contracts. Never use owner episodes or credentials as fixtures.
